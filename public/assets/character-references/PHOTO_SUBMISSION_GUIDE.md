# STREET LEGENDS — CHARACTER PHOTOGRAPH SUBMISSION GUIDE

To achieve maximum facial accuracy and realistic character likeness for the 5 fighters, organize reference photographs into the designated subfolders:

```
public/assets/character-references/
├── fighter-01/   (Character 1 - Male)
├── fighter-02/   (Character 2 - Male)
├── fighter-03/   (Character 3 - Male)
├── fighter-04/   (Character 4 - Female)
└── fighter-05/   (Character 5 - Female)
```

---

## Recommended Photo References per Fighter

For each individual, provide the following high-resolution image files:

1. **`front.jpg` — Frontal Portrait (Highest Priority)**
   - Straight-on eye-level view of the face.
   - Neutral facial expression (mouth closed, relaxed jaw).
   - Even, diffused lighting without harsh one-sided shadows or strong flash reflection.
   - Hair pulled back slightly or styled naturally without obscuring forehead, eyes, or ears.

2. **`left.jpg` & `right.jpg` — 90° Side Profile Portraits**
   - Exact 90° side angle profile.
   - Essential for sculpting the nose bridge, jawline angle, cheekbone depth, and ear positioning.

3. **`body.jpg` — Full-Body Standing Reference**
   - Head-to-toe full-body shot in a natural standing pose (or neutral A-pose).
   - Used to calibrate height ratio, shoulder width, arm span, and overall athletic build.

4. **Additional Reference Shots (Optional)**
   - 45° three-quarter angle portraits.
   - Detail photos of unique distinguishing features (e.g., facial hair style, tattoos, distinctive hair texture).

---

## Production & Privacy Guidelines
- **Confidentiality:** Original reference photographs stored in `public/assets/character-references/` are used exclusively for asset reconstruction and are strictly omitted from final production builds.
- **File formats supported:** `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp`.
